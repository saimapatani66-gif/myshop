Frequently Asked Questions

How can I find a product?

Use the search box at the top of the website or browse products by category.

Can I add products to my cart?

Yes. Select "Add to Cart" on a product and then open the cart to review the items.

Is checkout currently live?

The current checkout is a demo experience. Real payments, order processing and delivery require a backend and payment service.

Where can I read the policies?

Privacy, terms and refund information are available through the links in the footer.

Important Information

Some products and checkout features on this website are provided as demonstration content. Product availability, pricing, payment processing and delivery should be confirmed before any real purchase.
