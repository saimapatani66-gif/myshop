Refund & Return Policy

At My Shop, we want our customers to have a positive shopping experience. This policy explains how returns and refunds may be handled.

Returns

Customers may request a return if a product is damaged, defective, or significantly different from the product description.

Return Conditions

  • The product should be in reasonable condition.
  • The customer should contact us as soon as possible.
  • Proof of purchase or order details may be required.
  • Some products may not be eligible for return.

Refunds

If a return is approved, the refund method and processing time will depend on the payment method used for the order.
