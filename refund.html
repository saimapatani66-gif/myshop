Refund Policy

Information about returns, refunds and order cancellations.

1. Introduction

At My Shop, we aim to provide a smooth and reliable shopping experience. This Refund Policy explains the general conditions that may apply to returns, cancellations and refunds.

2. Order Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation of an order before it has been processed or shipped.

Once an order has been processed or shipped, cancellation may not be possible.

3. Returns

Return eligibility may depend on the product, its condition and the reason for the return.

  • The product should generally be unused and in suitable condition.
  • Original packaging and accessories may be required.
  • Return requests should be submitted within the applicable return period.
Return conditions may vary depending on the product and seller.

4. Damaged or Incorrect Products

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, please contact us as soon as possible with relevant order details.

Additional information or photographs may be requested to help review the issue.

5. Refunds

If a refund is approved, the refund method and processing time may depend on the original payment method and the relevant payment provider.

Refunds will only be processed after the applicable request has been reviewed and approved.

6. Non-Refundable Situations

Some products or situations may not qualify for a refund. This can include products that have been used, damaged after delivery, modified by the customer or returned without required accessories.

7. Processing Time

Refund and return processing times may vary depending on the order, shipping process and payment provider.

We will try to process approved requests as efficiently as reasonably possible.

8. Changes to This Policy

My Shop may update this Refund Policy when our products, services or business processes change. Any updated version will be published on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about a return or refund, please contact us through our Contact Us page.
